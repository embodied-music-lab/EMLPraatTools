# Ruling — kit relevance of the seven, and the drive plan

Fable, 26 August 2026. Follows the seven-answer memo. Names which items touch
the kit and how to drive them without rerunning everything.

## Six of the seven land in the kit. `.5` does not

The `.5` and coerced ruling lives in the CELL-CLASSIFICATION LAYER. It governs
what enters a table before any kernel runs, and its checks are the 7.7
driven-evidence fixture in `validate/`. No kit fixture contains such a cell, so
the kit is untouched by it.

## Four items add or restore compared rows

**3.9 — the Scheffe interval.** New quantities, new rows, base-R
first-principles oracle.

**`D-NODIFF` — all 720 rows convert from exempt to compared** once the point
differences print. The mean difference and the Hodges-Lehmann shift PRINT ON
EVERY PAIRWISE ROW, EVERY CORRECTION. Only the INTERVAL is Bonferroni-gated.

**`D-PAIRWISE-N` — all 400 rows compare** once `.stN` is assigned in every arm.

**`D-HEDGES` — the 24 rows should reach machine precision** once the exact
`lnGamma` correction lands. **If they do not, that is a finding, not a
tolerance.**

## Two items change the kit's accounting rules, not its comparisons

**`D-KW-ETA`** — the 80 eta-squared rows become a DOCUMENTED ABSENCE under the
quantity contract: a decided non-quantity with the decision cited, never an
exemption.

**`D-TWOWAY-PRECISION`** — the 18 rows get a NAMED PER-PROCEDURE TOLERANCE,
about 1e-8, bounded by Praat's report precision, with the reason stated in the
rule itself.

## The drive plan, per the standing scoping rule

**THE QUANTITY CONTRACT IS THE LIVING DOCUMENT.** Each punch item names exactly
the rows or rules it touches. As each build lands, DRIVE THOSE ROWS ONLY — the
item's delta is its acceptance evidence.

The full 630-run sweep runs AT GATES and at Fable's inspection, nowhere else.

**Nothing in the kit waits on anything.** Rows convert from excused to compared
as fixes arrive.

**THE BALANCE INVARIANT, CHECKED AT EVERY COMMIT:**

    compared + documented-absent + tolerance-bounded = total

so a row can never silently fall out of all three categories.
