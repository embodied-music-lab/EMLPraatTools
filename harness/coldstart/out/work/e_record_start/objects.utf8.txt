Table emlRecordMeta
== INFO ==
EML: recording started.

Every EML analysis, figure and table edit from now on is
added to one script. Finish with 'Stop recording and open'
to read it in an editor, or 'Stop recording and save' to
file it directly.

WHAT GOES INTO IT, AND WHAT DOES NOT:

    Recorded — anything you do through an EML command:
        analyses, figures, saves, and changes you make
        in EML's own 'EML: Edit Table...' window.

    Not recorded — a cell you type into Praat's own
        table window, and anything a script of your own
        does to your data.

An edit made in Praat's own table window can still be
got back by hand: open a script window and choose
Edit > Paste history. Praat writes out what you did in a
form that runs, and you can paste it into the recorded
script where it belongs. Work done by a script of your
own leaves no trace in either place.

To keep the whole session reproducible, edit your tables
through 'EML: Edit Table...' and they are recorded with
everything else.

Two Tables now sit in the Objects window:

    emlRecording_DO_NOT_REMOVE
        the steps — this IS the recording, and removing it
        ends the session, which is why it is named that way
    emlRecordMeta
        when it started and what it ran on

A third, 'eml-record-phrases', appears after the first step;
it is the shipped wording the script is written from. Leave
all three alone and they are cleaned up when you stop.

'Stop recording and open' puts the script in an editor and
keeps a review copy at:
