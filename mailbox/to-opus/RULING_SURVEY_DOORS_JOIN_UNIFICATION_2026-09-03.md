# Ruling — the survey module joins the doorway reconciliation

Fable, 3 September 2026, relaying Ian's direct ruling: "The survey
module should be part of the reconciliation between doorways."

## What this merges

The survey lane stops being a forever-separate track and folds into
the post-kit doors/unification round. Concretely, that round's scope
now includes:

- opening the survey-lane doors: the four deliberately-internal
  kernels (emlCronbachAlpha, emlAlphaInfluence, emlWilsonInterval,
  emlChiSquareIndependence) gain their registered menu entries and
  wizard pages, built under the same wizard-parity contract as every
  other door (guided route to the same analyses and options as the
  menu; nothing user-choosable hardcoded);
- resolving the wizardStub: the "Categorical association
  (chi-squared)" placeholder branch gets its real implementation in
  the same round, not as a separate favor;
- the registry additions this implies are made by the deliberate
  decision the registry's own header reserves — that decision is now
  SCHEDULED (it happens in the unification round) rather than open.

## What this does not change

- The survey lane's own gates still bind: staged builds, approved
  language, red-demoed refusals — folding into the reconciliation
  round sequences those gates inside it, it does not skip them.
- Timing: the unification round remains POST-KIT, and because
  opening these doors grows the public surface, it lands AFTER the
  freeze — the 42-row registry and Table S2 stay fixed for 1.0 and
  the paper, per the settlement principle. If Ian intends these
  doors pre-freeze, that is a new surface decision he would need to
  make explicitly; absent that, post-freeze is the reading.
- v162's new exemption behaves correctly by construction here: the
  moment these modules gain menu registrations, they mechanically
  lose their menu-less exemption and the door-chain reachability
  requirement snaps onto them. The check is already shaped for this
  ruling.

Fold into the unification round's scope now (a line in its punch
